Frequently asked questions

The camera doesn't open or the scanner is black.

Atithi QR needs camera access to read QR codes. Open the iPhone Settings app, choose Atithi QR and turn on Camera.

How do I change which weights are shown?

Tap the menu (three lines) at the top left, choose Settings, then turn Gross, Less or Net on or off. Single Weight Mode shows only the Net weight.

How do I turn off the beep or vibration?

Open Settings in the app and switch off Play Sound or Enable Vibration. If you hear no beep, also check that your iPhone isn't on silent and the volume is up.

How do I remove a scan?

Tap the red X next to a scanned item to delete it, or tap Clear All to remove every scan and start again.

What is Click to Scan mode?

With Enable Click Mode on, the app scans one code each time you tap the Click to Scan button, instead of scanning continuously.
