Privacy Policy

Atithi QR · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how the Atithi QR app ("the app") handles information. In short: the app does not collect, share or sell your personal data.

Camera

The app uses your iPhone's camera only to read QR codes. Camera images are processed on your device while you scan. They are not saved, uploaded or shared.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account or sign-up, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

The values from your scans (such as weights and scan times) and your app settings are stored locally on your device so the app can show your totals and history. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, and you can remove it any time by deleting scans in the app or deleting the app.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share your information with anyone. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and change the date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email contact@litbylines.com.
